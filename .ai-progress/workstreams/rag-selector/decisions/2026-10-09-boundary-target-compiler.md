## Decision

Isolate offline target compilation from all production scoring/training. Bind a
versioned overlay to complete immutable inputs. Keep existing flags excluded;
require explicit six-dimensional scope and distinct review witnesses for qualified
drafts. Verify witness file hashes but do not equate metadata with independence or
semantic correctness. Preserve original references and labels. All outputs remain
training_consumable=false, including synthetic qualification fixtures.

Use missed direct vs selected irrelevant only, normalized per-question pair weights
and distinct-slot budget. Qualify/protect existing selected direct first. Partial,
missing, uncertain and contradictory abstain; no direct-vs-direct preferences or
five-gold forcing. Source/default behavior yields proposals only.
